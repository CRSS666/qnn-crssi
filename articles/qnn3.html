BlanketCon '25 On The Horizonⁱ

Our gratuitous prime minister has announced that they,
through a mysterious source named 'Bluesky',
have found that an event named "BlanketCon '25"
is happening in the next monthⁱ.

It seems to be a convention about "mods", which seems
to mean things imported from the word of every author
in the convention.

Some of the mods are:

  • Snapper
    • A tool to make sorting pictures easeier
  • Hollow
    • Blocks and entities inspired by "hollow
      ambitions" from someone called Mojang.
  • Joy
    • Bed sheets, candles, and entities with
      LGBT tweaks
  • Sentient Pants
    • Apparently we have ThinkPant now.

Ploopy Fixed Windmill

It appears that the incredible Ploopy504 has appeared
in CRSS and fixed the windmill at their farm!

In an interview, they've told us that they came back to help Rhys
gather information about their house, and since they were back here
they decided to fix their windmill, too!

Blurry met up with them, and helped with resources they had available.
Blurry also gifted Ploopy an elytra and fireworks, as the last time
Ploopy was here was way before those existed.
